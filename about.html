About Us

Story of the Bakery

Founded in 1992 by John North, North Star Bakery began as a small family project dedicated to traditional European baking techniques. Over three decades later, John and his two daughters continue to craft fresh, honest sourdough breads and pastries every single morning with genuine care for our neighbors.

Sourcing Philosophy

We believe that uncompromised taste starts at the source. We partner directly with regional grain growers and family farms to bring wholesome, sustainable ingredients straight to your table.

  • Local Grains: Milled from certified organic whole grains grown by regional family farms.
  • Farm-Fresh Dairy: Fresh whole milk, cultured butter, and pure cream from pasture-raised cows.
  • Natural Fermentation: Slow 24-hour sourdough fermentation using wild yeast cultures with zero artificial additives.
