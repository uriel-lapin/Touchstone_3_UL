Our History

What began as a John North's baking hobby in the summer of 1992 quickly turned into a thriving business as .
word spread across the community. Starting with a single variety of sourdough bread, over the years
our expertise has expanded to include varieties of breads, pastries, cakes and sweets.
Today, our products are famous and shipped to markets fresh, state-wide every morning
Still owned and operated by John and his 2 daughters, the business continues to thrive
We are proud to serve the community, and hope to continue to serve to community for decades to come
and we invite you to stop by our shop to enjoy a warm, fresh slice with our family.
